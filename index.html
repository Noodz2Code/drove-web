Drove privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Drove is a journal for your trips, by car or by motorcycle. This policy says what the app does with your information. The short version: your trips stay on your phone, and Drove has no account, no advertising and no tracking.

Who we are

This policy covers the Drove app for Android, published on Google Play by Drove. For any question about this policy or about your information, write to drove.app.sm@gmail.com.

What Drove stores, and where

  • Your trips. When a trip is recording, Drove reads your location from your phone and saves the route, the times and the distance. This is saved on your phone only. Drove has no server and does not upload your trips.
  • Your settings, such as units and light or dark. On your phone only.

Location

  • Drove uses your location to show where you are on the map and to record your trips.
  • If you turn on automatic recording, Drove collects location data to record your trips automatically when your phone connects to Android Auto, even when the app is closed or not in use. Drove still reads your location only while a trip is being recorded. You can turn automatic recording off in Settings at any time.
  • Your location is not sent to us or to anyone else by Drove.

What leaves your phone

  • The map. To draw the map, Drove downloads map data from OpenFreeMap. Like any map, that request tells the map provider which area is being looked at, and your internet address. It does not include your trips.
  • A road picture, only if you share one. If you make a picture of a road and press Share, your phone sends it to the app or person you choose. Drove does not send it anywhere itself.

Nothing else leaves your phone because of Drove.

Keeping it safe

  • Your trips and settings are kept in Drove's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read.
  • Drove has no server, so your trips are not stored anywhere else.
  • Map data is downloaded over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

How long it is kept

  • Your trips are kept on your phone until you delete them, or until you uninstall Drove.

Backups and a new phone

  • Drove's data is not included in cloud backups.
  • When you set up a new phone directly from your old one, your phone can copy Drove's data across. That copy goes from phone to phone.
  • If you uninstall Drove, its trips are deleted.

What Drove does not do

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics and no tracking of what you do in the app.
  • No selling or sharing of your information.

Your choices

  • You can delete any trip, or all trips, in the app.
  • You can withdraw location permission, notification permission or automatic recording at any time, in the app or in your phone's settings.

Children

Drove is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on Drove's privacy policy web page, and its date will change.
